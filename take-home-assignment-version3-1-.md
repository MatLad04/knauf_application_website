# Take-Home Assignment

## Building Materials Digital Experience

### Working Student — Full Stack Developer

Thank you for your interest in joining our team.

This assignment is designed to understand how you approach an unfamiliar problem from beginning to end. We are interested not only in the final application, but also in how you investigate, ask questions, make assumptions, use available tools, and communicate your decisions.

There is no single correct solution.

---

## 1. The challenge

Imagine that a company in the building materials industry wants to improve the way people discover and understand its products and solutions.

Your task is to propose and develop a small digital experience that supports this objective.

Create a web application for discovering building material products.

A user should be able to:

1. View a list of products.
2. Search for products.
3. Filter products by relevant criteria.
4. Open a product and view detailed information.
 
You should decide which product categories, filters, and product details are most useful.
The application should be designed for a professional building materials company and should use an appropriate, consistent visual style.

---

## 2. Investigation

Before starting implementation, investigate the problem and its context.

Your investigation may include:

- The building materials industry.
- The types of products and solutions involved.
- Potential users and their needs.
- How people search for or compare technical products.
- Existing digital experiences in this area.
- The company, its products, its audience, and its visual communication.
- Relevant technical, accessibility, or usability considerations.

Decide for yourself what information is important to research.

---

## 3. The application

Build a small web-based prototype based on your investigation.

You may decide:

- What the application does.
- Which features are most important.
- Which data it uses.
- How users interact with it.
- Which technologies you use.
- Whether a backend or API is necessary.
- What is included in the first version.
- What is intentionally left out.

The application should be understandable and usable without a detailed explanation from you.

Please consider appropriate handling of:

- Empty states.
- Loading states.
- Errors.
- Invalid input.
- Different screen sizes.
- Accessibility.
- Maintainability.

You do not need to implement every possible feature. We encourage you to define a reasonable scope and explain your priorities.

---

## 4. Data and sources

You may use:

- Local or fictional data.
- A mock API.
- A backend that you create.
- Publicly available data.
- Another suitable approach.

If you use external information, please cite your sources.

Do not use confidential information or copy protected content without permission.

Please explain your data decisions and any limitations in your documentation.

---

## 5. Technology

You may choose the technologies you are most comfortable with.

You may use a framework, a library, or plain web technologies. You may also decide whether to create a backend.

There is no required programming language or framework.

Please focus on:

- A clear and maintainable solution.
- Appropriate technical decisions.
- A reasonable scope.
- A working and understandable result.

---

## 6. Use of AI tools

AI tools are allowed and encouraged.

You may use AI for research, brainstorming, design, implementation, debugging, testing, documentation, or code review.

However, you are responsible for understanding, reviewing, and testing everything you submit.
